How Portal Guanabara looks and why. The tokens live in
`packages/presentation-lake/src/Shared/foundations/tokens/colors.scss`; the icons in
`packages/presentation-lake/src/Shared/foundations/Icon/`. Storybook shows both:
**Foundations/Colors**, **Foundations/Icons**, **Journeys/Catalog/CatalogIcon**.

## 60 / 30 / 10

| Share | Role | Colour | Tokens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 60% | Canvas and surfaces | Névoa (misty off-white) and white | `--color-bg-*`, `--color-surface*` |
| 30% | Structure: site header and footer, hero bands, headings | Baía (deep bay blue) | `--color-structure-*`, `--color-text-heading` |
| 10% | Action: primary buttons, selected filters, active nav indicator | Soft Comlurb orange with **black** text | `--color-action-primary*`, `--color-selected-*`, `--color-accent*` |

Rules:

- One primary action per view. Orange is never used for decoration.
- Text on the soft orange is always black ink (8.83:1). Stronger oranges are kept for focus rings (`--color-focus-ring`, ≥3:1) and orange text on light tints.
- Comlurb colours are an homage to Rio's urban-cleaning orange and blue: colours only, no logos or brand marks.

## Meaning colours

Used only as small marks (icon fills, stripes, chips), always next to a text label.

| Concept | Mark | Line / text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project (iniciativa) — field action | soft orange `--color-kind-project` | `--color-kind-project-ink`; chips black on `--color-kind-project-chip` |
| Study (estudo) — research | soft purple `--color-kind-study` | `--color-kind-study-ink`; chips black on `--color-kind-study-chip` |
| Keyword categories | resíduo **golden yellow** (v0.9.29; brown before), ambiente teal, biota leaf green (a deeper green since v0.9.29, so yellow and green stay apart), processos físicos blue, socioambiental magenta | `--color-topic-*` (validated together for colour-vision deficiency) |
| Bay sectors | the sector's band: water soft orange, land soft purple | `--color-sector-*` |
| Status | alive green, in progress teal, ended grey | `--color-status-*` |

Charts stay single-hue (`--color-data-series-1`, água teal) and categories are never told apart by colour alone — with one exception since v0.8.1: the **kind** colours may split or paint bars and map markers, always with a legend and the counts in words (orange = initiatives, purple = studies).

**Estuda / atua (v0.8).** The kinds' colours also carry the two verbs of the promise on Início: in “Quem **estuda** e quem **atua** pela Baía de Guanabara” *estuda* is drawn in the research purple (`--color-kind-study`) and *atua* in the action orange (`--color-kind-project`). Copy says **atua/atuam** for initiatives (not *age/agem*) so the verb matches the colour everywhere. The title is one message with `<study>` and `<act>` rich-text tags, so every language colours its own verbs. **Since v0.9.17** each coloured word has a **black contour half as thick as the mark's islands** (0.495 of the mark's 20-unit grid, halved in v0.9.18: about 3px beside the 240px mark; the full thickness was too heavy), drawn outside the letters, and the title breaks after *pela* — “Quem estuda e quem atua pela / Baía de Guanabara, em um só lugar” (`<line>` tags in the message, so each language places its own break). Início's hero shows *Portal Guanabara* in white over the plain mark. **Since v0.9.19** the name is the band's largest words (up to 44px, the size the title had) with the same black contour as *estuda* and *atua*, and the title is one size down (up to 36px); the name and the title start at the same height, at the top (v0.9.20; v0.9.19 lowered the text). **Since v0.9.21** the band's content uses the whole width: the mark and the name are **centred between the window's left edge and the start of the text** (the first column runs from the edge to the text, the aside is centred in it), the text keeps at most 40rem, and a smaller column closes the row on the right (`--page-hero-max-width`, `--page-hero-padding-inline`, `--page-hero-columns` on `PageHero`). **Since v0.9.23** the three distances across the band are equal — from the window's left edge to the name, from the name to the text, and from the end of the text to the right edge (two columns sized to their content, the text at most 40rem, `space-evenly`, with a 32px least distance) — and the description is **justified**, so the text ends on one line (`--page-hero-description-align`). **Since v0.9.28** the name sits **under the mark in two lines of capitals — PORTAL / GUANABARA** — centred, 34px with a little letter spacing so GUANABARA is about as wide as the mark, each line with the same black contour; the mark starts level with the title. Above the mark in one large line it competed with the title beside it. The two lines come from the `brand` message (first word, then the rest), with a space between them for assistive tech.

## Icons

- 24×24 grid, 1.75px rounded line, duotone: `tint` (~18–35% fill), `accent` and `secondary` highlight fills, thin `detail` lines (map shorelines), `solid` marks.
- Sizes: `xs` 16 (inline facts, chips), `sm` 20 (pills, labels), `md` 24 (titles), `lg` 32 (card icons, stat tiles), `xl` 48 (duo cards, sector tiles, entry header).
- Groups: interface (search, arrows, calendar, map-pin, institution…), the bay mark (`bay`), the kind duo (`kind-project` — a hand holding the bay's water; `kind-study` — a lens over the bay's water; see v0.6 below), the `text-size` glyph, the Opções set (v0.9.18: `translate` — an “A” bubble and a “文” bubble — and `zoom-in` — a lens with a plus — head its two sections; the button keeps `sliders`: a combined glyph tried in v0.9.18 was less clear and was dropped in v0.9.19), five bay sectors, twenty keyword glyphs and ten mechanism glyphs (v0.9.18).
- The bay is redrawn by hand over a trace of the real shoreline, with Ilha do Governador, Ilha do Fundão and Paquetá; island channels are widened so they read at 24px. Sector glyphs stack interior → transição → entrada, plus the open sea below the mouth.
- Catalogue concepts are drawn through `CatalogIcon` (`kind`, `sector`, `keyword`), which owns their colours. Unknown sectors fall back to a map pin; unknown keywords to a neutral tag.

## Official mark (v0.6; the G with the protected water since v0.9.26)

`Shared/foundations/BrandMark` is the Portal Guanabara mark since v0.9.26: **a G that protects the bay's water**, with two small fish — **orange for who acts** (`--color-kind-project`), **purple for who studies** (`--color-kind-study`) — with black eyes (`nevoa-950`; the G's colour in v0.9.26). Since v0.9.27 the G carries a **black contour** (`nevoa-950`, 0.8 units each side: the width of the name's contour on Início, about 3px at 240px), drawn as the G's ring and bar widened underneath, so the white G reads against the band and the water. The water (`agua-400`) has a wavy surface level with the G's bar. The G is light (`nevoa-50`) on dark backgrounds (`tone="on-dark"`, the default: header and Início's band) and dark (`baia-800`) on light ones (`tone="on-light"`). Chosen after a round of sketches (the bay tile, A Entrada, G with the bay's outline, a blue ball, the water with waves): the mark is no longer drawn like an icon (flat shapes; its only contour is the G's), while the bay stays the duotone `Icon` everywhere else. The tab icon (`public/favicon.svg`) is the G and its water on the header's blue, without the fish. The bay tile of v0.6–v0.9.25 (wordmark included) is gone, with its `geometry.ts`.

## Kind icons (v0.6)

The bottle and the drop are gone: both kinds now hold **the bay's water** — the outline of Guanabara with Ilha do Governador as a hole, traced from the same shoreline. Iniciativa: a hand holding the bay (acting for Guanabara). Estudo: a lens over the bay (research about Guanabara). The water is drawn with the thin `detail` line so it reads at 16px.

## Layout: cards in 1 / 2 / 3 columns (v0.5)

Every collection of cards uses the `CardGrid` primitive (`Shared/primitives/CardGrid`):

| Viewport | Columns | Content width (`--layout-max-width`) |
|---|---|---|
| < 720px (phones) | 1 | full width |
| 720–1599px (tablets, laptops such as a MacBook) | 2 | 1120px |
| ≥ 1600px (large screens) | 3 | 1440px |

Cards in a row stretch to the same height. On Início, Instituições and Municípios are explore cards like the kinds since v0.9.20 (two tiles of key numbers before). The initiatives × studies duo cards follow their own width (container query): under 26rem the arrow goes, under 19rem the icon and number sit on top of the words, so two cards side by side at 120% never overlap. Pairs (the initiatives × studies duo, the highlights) cap at 2 (`max-columns="2"`). The five sector tiles (v0.9.22) sit in **two rows, three and two, the second centred**, at the explore cards' height with the same framed xl icon (68px with its tile), so the section reads as a sibling of Explorar o catálogo; since v0.9.23 each keeps the width it had when the five shared one row (a fifth of the row; at least 14.5rem since v0.9.24) with **32px between them**, the group centred and the second row under the first one's gaps; on tablets two per row, on phones one.
