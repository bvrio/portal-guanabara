# Product Model

```text
App
  ↓
Journey
  ↓
Feature
  ↓
Page
```

## App

Major interaction surface.

## Journey

Cohesive product experience and the central Frontend/BFF organizational unit.

## Feature

Meaningful capability usage on the product within a Journey.

## Page

Concrete user-facing experience.

## Domain

Business ownership boundary and source of truth. Domains are implemented by Backend systems; BFFs may adapt and orchestrate Domain capabilities without owning the authoritative business truth.
